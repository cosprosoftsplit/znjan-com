## Sunset Timing at Žnjan

Žnjan faces **south-southwest**, giving you wide-angle sunset views over the Adriatic with the islands of Brač and Šolta on the horizon. The sun doesn't set directly over the sea (it dips behind the Marjan hill to the west), but the sky colors are spectacular.

### Sunset Times by Month

| Month | Sunset | Golden Hour Starts |
|-------|--------|--------------------|
| **June** | ~20:30 | ~19:30 |
| **July** | ~20:25 | ~19:25 |
| **August** | ~19:50 | ~18:50 |
| **September** | ~19:00 | ~18:00 |
| **May & October** | ~19:45 / ~18:00 | ~18:45 / ~17:00 |

**Tip:** Arrive at least 30 minutes before sunset for the best light. The golden hour glow on the palm-lined promenade is unforgettable.

## Best Sunset Spots

### 1. Casa Sol Terrace (Best for Dining)

The restaurant's outdoor terrace faces west-southwest — the single best spot for a sunset dinner. Book a table for 19:00–19:30 in summer to catch the full show.

### 2. The Promenade (Best for Walking)

The 2 km palm-lined promenade is stunning during golden hour. Start at the eastern end (near the amphitheatre) and walk west as the sun drops. The palms, warm light, and Adriatic backdrop make every phone photo look professional.

### 3. Amphitheatre Steps (Best for Sitting)

The amphitheatre with its steel dome sits at the eastern end of the beach. The stepped seating faces the water and catches the sunset light beautifully. Free, open, and perfect for couples.

### 4. Beach Club Daybeds (Best for Luxury)

Watching the sunset from a daybed at **Taboo** or **Palma** with a cocktail in hand is peak Žnjan. Order a sundowner cocktail around 19:00 and settle in.

### 5. Main Beach Waterline (Best for Photos)

Walk to the water's edge on the main beach. The wet pebbles reflect the sunset colors, and you get the full panorama — sea, islands, sky. Bring a low-angle camera for dramatic shots.

## Evening Activities

### Sunset Swim (Free)

The water temperature stays above 24°C from June through September. An evening swim as the sun sets is one of the best free experiences in Split. The beach is less crowded after 18:00, and the water glows amber.

### Promenade Stroll (Free)

After sunset, the promenade comes alive. The LED lighting along the path creates a warm, resort-like atmosphere. Walk the full 2 km and browse the beach club terraces.

### Beach Club Evenings

- **Taboo Beach Club:** DJ sets, their signature Utopia party with aerialists and saxophonists
- **Palma Beach Club:** Cocktails and upbeat music until late
- **Mistral Beach Club:** More refined evening vibe, craft cocktails on the deck

### Amphitheatre Events

The amphitheatre with its distinctive steel dome hosts concerts, film screenings, and cultural events throughout summer. Check local listings — events are often free.

### Evening Run or Cycle

The promenade is flat, well-lit, and largely car-free — perfect for an evening jog or bike ride. The route from Bačvice to Žnjan (4 km) is popular with local runners.

## Evening Photography Tips

1. **Golden hour** (30 min before sunset) — warm light on faces, palms, and buildings
2. **Blue hour** (15–30 min after sunset) — deep blue sky, city lights beginning to glow
3. **Best angles:** Face west from the promenade; use palms as foreground framing
4. **Reflections:** Wet pebbles at the waterline create mirror-like sunset reflections
5. **Silhouettes:** Stand at the water's edge and shoot people walking the promenade against the sunset sky
6. **Night mode:** After dark, the illuminated promenade and beach club lights make excellent long-exposure shots

## Practical Tips

1. **Bring a light layer** — evenings cool down by 5–8°C after sunset, especially with sea breeze
2. **Mosquitoes** appear at dusk near green areas — bring repellent
3. **Beach clubs serve until late** — you can order food and drinks well past sunset
4. **The promenade is well-lit** — safe for evening walks
5. **Uber/Bolt home** — have the app ready if you're staying for a late evening. Last buses run around 23:00
6. **Sunset from the water** — if you're a strong swimmer, swimming out 20–30 meters gives you an incredible view of the sunset reflecting off the promenade
