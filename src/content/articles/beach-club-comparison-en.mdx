## Quick Comparison Table

All 7 venues are **new pavilion buildings** opened as part of Žnjan's €45.5M transformation in 2025. They line the palm-fringed promenade and waterfront — each with a different personality.

| Venue | Type | Cuisine | Price | Best For |
|-------|------|---------|-------|----------|
| **Palma Beach Club** | Beach club | Mediterranean, cocktails | $$$ | Daytime vibes, groups |
| **Taboo Beach Club** | Luxury club | Mediterranean-Asian fusion | $$$$ | VIP experience, parties |
| **Mistral Beach Club** | Upscale club | Mediterranean, sushi | $$$$ | Fine dining, couples |
| **Casa Sol** | Restaurant | Mediterranean | $$$ | Sunset dinners, dates |
| **Central Beach** | Restaurant-bar | Mediterranean, seafood | $$$ | Casual waterfront dining |
| **MIMI** | Italian clubino | Italian, pasta, pizza | $$$ | Italian food lovers |
| **GAL Split** | Cafe-lounge | Mediterranean, breakfast | $$ | All-day casual, breakfast |

## The Beach Clubs (Party + Lounging)

### Palma Beach Club — The Social Hub

Palma is where the energy is. Positioned along the promenade with direct beach access, it attracts a mix of locals and tourists who want cocktails, music, and Adriatic views without the ultra-luxury price tag.

- **Vibe:** Lively, social, upbeat music
- **Sunbeds:** Available with table service
- **Cocktails:** Signature cocktail menu
- **Music:** DJs on weekends, upbeat playlists daily
- **Best time:** Afternoon into sunset
- **Price level:** $$$ — mid-premium
- **Contact:** [palmabeachclub.com](https://palmabeachclub.com/) | [@palmabeachclub](https://instagram.com/palmabeachclub)

**Best for:** Groups of friends, social afternoons, people who want the beach club experience without VIP-level prices.

### Taboo Beach Club — The Luxury Experience

Taboo is Žnjan's most exclusive venue. Think premium daybeds, cabanas, VIP lounges, and their signature **Utopia party** with aerialists, live DJs, and saxophonists. The Mediterranean-Asian fusion menu matches the ambitious vibe.

- **Vibe:** Upscale, Ibiza-inspired, see-and-be-seen
- **Sunbeds:** Premium daybeds and private cabanas
- **Cocktails:** Premium spirits, bottle service available
- **Music:** Live DJs, saxophonists, themed events
- **Best time:** All day — different energy from morning to late night
- **Price level:** $$$$ — premium
- **Contact:** [taboo-beach.com](https://www.taboo-beach.com/) | [@taboobeachclub.split](https://instagram.com/taboobeachclub.split)

**Best for:** Couples wanting luxury, celebrations, visitors who love Ibiza/Mykonos-style beach clubs.

### Mistral Beach Club — The Refined Choice

Part of the **Radisson Blu Resort & Spa** complex, Mistral combines hotel-quality service with a genuine beach club atmosphere. The sushi menu sets it apart — plus Mediterranean and international dishes served right to your lounger or cabana.

- **Vibe:** Sophisticated, relaxed luxury, Dalmatian soul
- **Sunbeds:** Sun loungers and daybeds on pebble beach and wooden deck
- **Food:** Full restaurant — sushi, Mediterranean, international
- **Music:** Live DJ performances, mellower than Taboo
- **Best time:** Lunch through sunset
- **Price level:** $$$$ — premium
- **Contact:** [mistralbeachclub.com](https://mistralbeachclub.com/) | [@mistralbeachclub](https://instagram.com/mistralbeachclub)

**Best for:** Foodies, sushi lovers, couples, hotel guests wanting beach-side fine dining.

## The Restaurants (Dining Focus)

### Casa Sol — Sunset Dinner Spot

Casa Sol is a refined Mediterranean restaurant and lounge. No loud DJs here — just elegant seaside dining with excellent views. It's the place for a proper sit-down meal, especially at sunset.

- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean — fish, seafood, grilled meats
- **Vibe:** Relaxed, elegant, romantic
- **Best time:** Sunset dinner (book ahead in summer)
- **Price level:** $$$ — mid-premium
- **Contact:** [casasol.hr](https://www.casasol.hr/) | [@casasol.split](https://instagram.com/casasol.split)

**Best for:** Romantic dinners, couples, anyone who wants great food without the club atmosphere.

### Central Beach — Waterfront All-Rounder

Central Beach is the most versatile venue at Žnjan. Right on the waterfront (not the promenade), it works for morning coffee, a casual lunch, or seafood dinner. Table reservations are available — unusual for beach restaurants.

- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean, seafood focus
- **Vibe:** Casual, contemporary, family-friendly
- **Best time:** Any time — from morning coffee to dinner
- **Price level:** $$$ — mid-range
- **Contact:** [centralbeachsplit.com](https://centralbeachsplit.com/) | [@centralbeachsplit](https://instagram.com/centralbeachsplit)

**Best for:** Families, casual diners, people who want beachfront food without the beach club markup.

### MIMI Italian Clubino — La Dolce Vita

MIMI brings authentic Italian cuisine to Žnjan. Fresh **handmade pasta daily**, wood-fired pizzas, curated wines, and craft cocktails in a warm, elegantly styled space. Part of Split's **F Group** family (Terminal F, Fabrique).

- **Cuisine:** Italian — fresh pasta, wood-fired pizza, wine
- **Vibe:** Intimate, warm, Italian-inspired
- **Best time:** Lunch or dinner
- **Price level:** $$$ — mid-premium
- **Contact:** [fgroup.hr](https://www.fgroup.hr/) | [@mimi_italian_clubino](https://instagram.com/mimi_italian_clubino)

**Best for:** Italian food lovers, pasta enthusiasts, wine-and-dine evenings, anyone craving pizza.

## The Cafe (All-Day Casual)

### GAL Split — Morning to Midnight

GAL is the most casual and approachable venue at Žnjan, and the best value. Open from breakfast through late evening, it's perfect for those who want good food and coffee without the beach club premium. The **Costa Coffee partnership** means proper espresso.

- **Cuisine:** Breakfast (shakshuka, French toast), artisan pizza, Mediterranean mains
- **Vibe:** Modern, relaxed, design-forward
- **Hours:** Mon–Thu 8:00–23:00, Fri–Sun 8:00–24:00 (kitchen: breakfast 8:00–12:30, lunch/dinner 13:00–22:00)
- **Best time:** Breakfast or afternoon coffee
- **Price level:** $$ — most affordable on the promenade
- **Contact:** [gal-split.com](https://gal-split.com/) | [@gal_split](https://instagram.com/gal_split)

**Best for:** Budget-conscious visitors, breakfast seekers, digital nomads, anyone who wants coffee and food without high beach club prices.

## How to Choose: Decision Guide

**Want to party?** → Taboo (luxury) or Palma (social)

**Want great food?** → Mistral (sushi + Mediterranean), MIMI (Italian), or Casa Sol (Mediterranean)

**On a budget?** → GAL Split ($$, all-day menu) or bring your own towel and picnic

**With kids?** → Central Beach (casual, waterfront) or GAL Split (relaxed, good breakfast)

**Romantic dinner?** → Casa Sol (sunset) or Mistral (refined dining)

**Just coffee?** → GAL Split (Costa Coffee, €2–4)

**Italian craving?** → MIMI (fresh pasta and wood-fired pizza daily)

## Practical Tips

1. **Reservations recommended** for dinner at Casa Sol, Mistral, and MIMI — especially in July and August
2. **Minimum spend** applies at premium beach clubs (Taboo, Palma, Mistral) — your daybed cost is typically a minimum food/drink order
3. **All venues are new** — opened 2025 as part of Žnjan's transformation, so facilities are modern and clean
4. **Free beach access** — you don't need to book a venue to enjoy the beach. Bring a towel and lie on the pebbles for free
5. **Walk the promenade first** — all 7 venues are within a 10-minute walk. Stroll past them all before deciding
6. **Follow on Instagram** — most venues post daily specials, events, and menus on their Instagram accounts
