## How Far Is the Airport from Žnjan Beach?

Split Airport (SPU) is located in **Kaštela**, about **24 km west** of Žnjan Beach. The drive takes **25–35 minutes** via the coastal road, though summer traffic through the Kaštela towns can push this to 40–50 minutes.

| Option | Price | Time | Best For |
|--------|-------|------|----------|
| **Promet Bus 37** | €1.50–3 | ~60 min | Budget travellers |
| **Airport shuttle** | €9 | 30–40 min | Solo mid-budget |
| **Bolt** | €30–45 | 25–30 min | Best value door-to-door |
| **Uber** | €35–50 | 25–30 min | App convenience |
| **Taxi** | €40–55 | 25–35 min | No app needed |
| **Rental car** | From €15/day | 25–30 min | Multi-day stays |

## Option 1: Public Bus (Cheapest — €1.50)

**Promet Split line 37** runs from the airport through the Kaštela towns to Split. It's the cheapest option but the slowest.

- **Price:** €1.50 via the Promet app, €3 from the driver
- **Frequency:** Every ~20 minutes on weekdays
- **Hours:** 04:00–23:45 daily
- **Journey time:** About 60 minutes to the Sukošan terminus in eastern Split

**How it works:** Take bus 37 into Split, then transfer to **bus 8 or 15** to reach Žnjan Beach. Total travel time including the transfer is approximately 75–90 minutes.

**Tip:** Download the Promet Split app to buy tickets at the cheaper €1.50 price. Tickets must be validated via QR after boarding — failure to validate risks a €20 fine.

## Option 2: Airport Shuttle Bus (€9)

**Pleso Prijevoz** operates the official airport shuttle to Split's main bus station.

- **Price:** €9 one way
- **Departure:** Timed to flight arrivals, usually 20 minutes after landing
- **Journey time:** 30–40 minutes
- **Drop-off:** Split Main Bus Station (Obala Kneza Domagoja 12)
- **Tickets:** Buy online at plesoprijevoz.hr or from the driver

**From the bus station to Žnjan:** The bus station is about 6 km from Žnjan Beach. Take **bus 15** (stops right at the bus station) or a quick Uber/Bolt (€6–9, 10 minutes).

**Best for:** Solo travellers on a mid-range budget. The €9 + €2 bus combo gets you to Žnjan for about €11 total.

## Option 3: Bolt (Best Value Door-to-Door)

**Bolt** is generally the cheapest ride-hailing option in Split and operates at the airport.

- **Price:** €30–45 to Žnjan Beach (varies by demand)
- **Journey time:** 25–30 minutes
- **Pickup:** Outside the terminal on the main road

**Tip:** Download the Bolt app before your trip. If you're travelling with 2–3 people, the per-person cost (€10–15 each) makes it comparable to the shuttle bus — and you go straight to the beach.

## Option 4: Uber

**Uber** also operates at Split Airport with UberX, Comfort, XL, and Van options.

- **Price:** €35–50 to Žnjan Beach
- **Journey time:** 25–30 minutes
- **Pickup:** Outside the terminal

**Uber vs Bolt:** Bolt is typically €5–10 cheaper for the same route. Both apps work well in Split. Surge pricing applies during peak summer months for both.

## Option 5: Taxi (No App Needed)

Metered taxis wait outside the airport terminal.

- **Price:** €40–55 to Žnjan Beach
- **Base fare:** ~€2.40 plus ~€1.10 per km
- **Journey time:** 25–35 minutes
- **Surcharges:** Night, weekend, and holiday rates may apply

**Tip:** Agree on the price before getting in, or insist the meter is running. Airport taxis can sometimes overcharge tourists.

## Option 6: Rental Car

Split Airport has an extensive car rental center with both international and local operators.

**Available companies:** Sixt, Europcar, Hertz, Avis, Budget, Enterprise, and several local agencies.

- **Price:** From about €15/day (off-season economy) to €50–80/day in peak summer
- **Journey time:** 25–30 minutes to Žnjan
- **Best for:** Visitors staying multiple days who want to explore beyond Split

**Parking at Žnjan:** The beach has **530 underground** and **451 outdoor** parking spaces (~981 total). Paid parking is in effect from July 16. The underground garage keeps your car cool in summer heat.

## Train — Not Available Yet

There is currently **no train service to Split Airport**. A rail extension is under planning (feasibility study completed as of 2026), but no construction date has been confirmed.

## Best Strategy by Budget

### Budget (€3–11)
Take **bus 37** from the airport to Split (€1.50–3), then **bus 15** to Žnjan (€1.50). Total: about €3–5. Or take the **Pleso shuttle** (€9) + bus 15 (€2) for €11 total.

### Mid-Range (€15–25 per person)
Split a **Bolt** with 2–3 travel companions. At €30–45 total, that's €10–15 per person for a comfortable door-to-door ride.

### Convenience (€35–55)
**Bolt** or **Uber** directly to the beach. No transfers, no waiting.

### Multi-Day (€15+/day)
**Rent a car** at the airport. Drive to Žnjan in 25 minutes. Use the underground garage for covered parking.

## Quick Tips

1. **Download Bolt and Uber** before landing — both work at Split Airport
2. **Summer traffic warning:** The coastal road through Kaštela gets congested July–August. Allow extra time
3. **Bus 37 to bus 15** is the cheapest combo (€3 total) but takes 75–90 minutes
4. **Book rental cars early** — summer prices spike and availability drops
5. **The shuttle drops you at the bus station**, not the beach. You'll need a second transfer
6. **Parking charges** at Žnjan Beach apply from mid-July — budget €5–10/day for parking if driving
