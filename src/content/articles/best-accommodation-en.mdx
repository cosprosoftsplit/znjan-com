## Overview: Staying Near Žnjan

Žnjan Beach sits on Split's eastern coast, about 4 km from the Old Town. The area has transformed from a quiet residential neighbourhood into a **beachfront destination** with several hotels within walking distance of the beach.

| Hotel | Stars | Walk to Beach | Price Range | Best For |
|-------|-------|--------------|-------------|----------|
| **Radisson Blu Resort & Spa** | 4★ | On the beach | €150–350 | Luxury, beach club access |
| **Hotel Amphora** | 4★ | 5 min | €100–200 | Families, value |
| **Hotel More** | 4★ | 10 min | €120–250 | Boutique, sea views |
| **Hotel Fanat** | 3★ | 8 min | €60–120 | Budget, simple |
| **Mövenpick Hotel** | 5★ | 3 min | €200–400+ | Ultra-luxury (2026) |
| **Airbnb / Apartments** | — | 2–15 min | €50–150 | Self-catering, groups |

## Luxury: Radisson Blu Resort & Spa

The **flagship hotel at Žnjan** — literally on the beach. The Radisson Blu is the only hotel with direct beachfront access and its own beach club (**Mistral Beach Club**).

- **Location:** Directly on the Žnjan coast, at the western end of the promenade
- **Rooms:** Modern resort rooms and suites with Adriatic views
- **Facilities:** Spa, indoor pool, Mistral Beach Club, restaurant, fitness centre
- **Beach access:** Walk out the door and you're on the beach
- **Price:** €150–350/night depending on season and room type
- **Instagram:** [@radissonblusplit](https://instagram.com/radissonblusplit)

**Best for:** Visitors who want luxury beachfront accommodation with resort amenities. The Mistral Beach Club access is a major perk — sushi, cocktails, and daybeds steps from your room.

## Mid-Range: Hotel Amphora

**Hotel Amphora** is a well-established 4-star hotel just a short walk from Žnjan Beach. Popular with families and couples seeking comfort without the luxury price tag.

- **Location:** ~5 minutes walk to the beach
- **Rooms:** Comfortable rooms and suites, some with sea views
- **Facilities:** Restaurant, bar, parking
- **Price:** €100–200/night
- **Instagram:** [@amphora_hotel_split](https://instagram.com/amphora_hotel_split)

**Best for:** Families, couples, and visitors wanting a reliable mid-range hotel close to the beach.

## Mid-Range: Hotel More

A boutique-style **4-star hotel** with character. Hotel More sits slightly further from the beach but offers excellent sea views and a quieter atmosphere.

- **Location:** ~10 minutes walk to the beach
- **Rooms:** Boutique rooms with character
- **Facilities:** Restaurant, terrace with sea views
- **Price:** €120–250/night

**Best for:** Couples and solo travellers who prefer boutique hotels over large resorts.

## Budget: Hotel Fanat

**Hotel Fanat** is the most affordable hotel near Žnjan. It's simple and functional — a clean room, friendly staff, and a short walk to the beach.

- **Location:** ~8 minutes walk to the beach
- **Rooms:** Simple, clean rooms
- **Facilities:** Basic — reception, breakfast
- **Price:** €60–120/night

**Best for:** Budget travellers who want a proper hotel room without paying resort prices. Spend the savings at the beach clubs.

## Apartments & Airbnb

The Žnjan neighbourhood is full of **private apartments** available on Airbnb, Booking.com, and local rental sites. This is often the best-value option, especially for groups and families.

### What to Expect
- **Price:** €50–150/night for a 1-2 bedroom apartment
- **Facilities:** Kitchen, washing machine, Wi-Fi, often a balcony
- **Location:** Many are within 5–10 minutes walk of the beach
- **Availability:** Book early for July–August — popular apartments sell out months in advance

### Tips for Booking Apartments Near Žnjan
1. **Search for "Žnjan" or "Split east"** on Airbnb/Booking.com — don't just search "Split" or you'll see mostly Old Town listings
2. **Check the map** — aim for addresses on or near Šetalište Pape Ivana Pavla II (the beach road)
3. **Look for parking** if you're renting a car — not all apartments include parking
4. **Read recent reviews** — focus on reviews from the last 6 months (post-renovation)
5. **Supermarkets nearby:** Konzum and Studenac are within walking distance
6. **Bus access:** Lines 8 and 15 connect Žnjan to the city centre every 15–20 minutes

### Best Neighbourhoods for Apartments
- **Žnjan proper** — closest to the beach (2–5 min walk), highest prices
- **Trstenik** — one neighbourhood west, 10–15 min walk to beach, good value
- **Split 3** — inland neighbourhood, 10–15 min walk, most affordable, local vibe

## How to Choose

**Money is no object?** → Radisson Blu (or Mövenpick when it opens)

**Comfortable but reasonable?** → Hotel Amphora (families) or Hotel More (couples)

**Budget-friendly hotel?** → Hotel Fanat

**Best value overall?** → Apartment/Airbnb in Žnjan (especially for groups of 3+)

**Multi-day beach holiday?** → Radisson Blu (walk out to beach) or a beachfront apartment

## Booking Tips

1. **Book 2–3 months ahead** for July–August stays — Žnjan is increasingly popular
2. **Compare Booking.com vs Airbnb** — hotels are often cheaper on Booking.com; apartments vary
3. **Consider shoulder season** (May–June, September–October) — prices drop 30–50%, weather is still great, beaches less crowded
4. **Half-board at hotels** can save money vs eating out every meal — compare the rates
5. **Airport transfers** — some hotels offer pickup from Split Airport. Ask when booking (or see our [airport transfer guide](/en/articles/split-airport-to-znjan-beach/))
