## Why Žnjan Instead of Bačvice?

Most cruise passengers heading to the beach walk to **Bačvice** — it's close to the port (15 minutes on foot). But Bačvice is small, gets crowded fast, and has limited facilities.

**Žnjan Beach is worth the extra 10-minute bus ride:**

| | Bačvice | Žnjan |
|---|---------|-------|
| Distance from port | 1.5 km (walk) | 5 km (bus) |
| Beach length | ~200 m | **2+ km** |
| Facilities | Basic | **7 new restaurants, beach clubs, showers, parking** |
| Crowding | Very crowded | More space per person |
| Renovation | Old | **€45.5M transformation (2025)** |
| Vibe | Local hangout | **Modern resort-style** |

## Getting from the Cruise Port to Žnjan

Split's cruise ships dock at the **Port of Split** (Gat Sv. Duje or the outer breakwater berths), right next to the Old Town. The port is less than 1 km from Diocletian's Palace.

### By Bus (Best Value — €1.50)

**Bus line 15** runs directly from the ferry/cruise port area to Žnjan.

- **Stop:** "Trajektna Luka" (ferry port) — right near where you disembark
- **Price:** ~€1.50 (buy from the driver or via the Promet app)
- **Journey time:** 20–25 minutes
- **Frequency:** Every 20–30 minutes
- **Get off at:** "Žnjan" stop — 5-minute walk to the beach

**Bus line 8** also serves Žnjan from the city centre.

### By Taxi or Uber (Fastest — €9–12)

- **Price:** €9–12 from the port to Žnjan Beach
- **Time:** 6–10 minutes
- **Tip:** Uber and Bolt are both available in Split and typically cheaper than port taxis

### On Foot (Scenic — 45–55 min)

Walk the coastal promenade: Port → Riva → Bačvice → Firule → Trstenik → Žnjan. It's flat, scenic, and passes several smaller beaches. But for time-limited cruise passengers, the bus is smarter.

## How Much Time Do You Have?

Most cruise ships in Split dock for **8–11 hours**, typically arriving at 07:00–09:00 and departing at 16:00–20:00.

Here's how to plan your day:

### Sample Itinerary: Old Town + Beach Day (10 hours)

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| **08:00** | Ship arrives. Walk to Diocletian's Palace (10 min) |
| **08:15–10:30** | Explore the Old Town — palace, cathedral, peristyle, underground halls |
| **10:30** | Grab coffee at a Riva café |
| **11:00** | Take bus 15 to Žnjan (20 min) |
| **11:30–16:00** | Beach time — swim, sunbathe, lunch at a beach restaurant |
| **16:00** | Bus back to port (20 min) or Uber (10 min) |
| **16:30** | Back at the port — browse the market stalls |
| **17:00–18:00** | Ship departs |

### Sample Itinerary: Beach-Focused Day (10 hours)

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| **08:00** | Ship arrives. Quick walk through Diocletian's Palace |
| **08:45** | Bus 15 or Uber to Žnjan |
| **09:15–16:00** | Full beach day — breakfast at GAL Split, swimming, promenade walk, lunch |
| **16:00** | Return to port |
| **16:30–17:00** | Last-minute shopping near the port |

## What to Bring from the Ship

You don't need to pack much — Žnjan has everything:

- **Swimsuit and towel** (some ships lend beach towels — check)
- **Sunscreen** (essential June–September)
- **Cash and card** — Croatia uses the **Euro** since 2023. Cards accepted at all restaurants
- **Phone with Uber/Bolt** — for a quick return to the port
- **Water shoes** — the beach is pebble, not sand (optional but helpful)

**You don't need:**
- Food — 7 restaurants on the promenade (from €8 pizza to €25 seafood)
- Snorkel gear — bring your own if that is part of your beach plan
- Sunbed — available at beach clubs (€15–50 depending on venue)

## What Žnjan Offers Cruise Passengers

Žnjan was completely rebuilt in 2025 with **€45.5 million** in investment. Everything is new:

- **2+ km of beach** — Split's largest
- **7 pavilion restaurants and beach clubs** — from budget-friendly GAL Split to luxury Taboo Beach Club
- **Free showers** along the beach
- **Palm-lined promenade** — perfect for walking
- **Amphitheatre** with a steel dome for events
- **Sports zone** — beach volleyball, cycling path
- **Kids area** with playground (600 m²) and shallow water

## Money-Saving Tips for Cruise Passengers

1. **Skip the ship's shore excursion** — a €1.50 bus ticket gets you to a world-class beach. No need to pay €50+ for an organized tour
2. **Eat at GAL Split** — the most affordable option on the promenade ($$, breakfast from €5, pizza from €8)
3. **Bring your own towel** — lie on the pebbles for free instead of renting a sunbed
4. **Take the bus, not a taxi** — bus 15 runs directly from the port for €1.50
5. **Don't exchange money** — Croatia uses the Euro. Pay by card everywhere

## Important: Don't Miss Your Ship

**Return to the port at least 30–60 minutes before departure time.** The ship will not wait for passengers on self-organized excursions.

- Know your ship's exact departure time (check the daily programme)
- Set a phone alarm for your return time
- Have Uber/Bolt ready as backup — a taxi takes only 10 minutes if you're running late
- Bus 15 runs every 20–30 minutes — don't rely on catching the very last possible bus

## Žnjan vs Other Shore Excursion Options

| Option | Time Needed | Cost | Highlights |
|--------|------------|------|------------|
| **Žnjan Beach day** | 4–6 hours | €5–50 | Beach, restaurants, swimming |
| **Old Town only** | 2–3 hours | Free–€15 | Palace, cathedral, markets |
| **Krka Waterfalls** | Full day | €50–80 | Nature, swimming, waterfalls |
| **Hvar Island** | Full day | €40–70 | Ferry + island exploration |
| **Trogir day trip** | 3–4 hours | €5–10 | UNESCO old town |

**Our recommendation:** Combine Old Town (morning) + Žnjan Beach (afternoon) for the perfect Split day. You get culture, beach, food, and swimming in one port call.
